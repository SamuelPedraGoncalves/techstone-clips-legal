Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. What we collect

When you sign in with TikTok, the app receives only the access and refresh tokens needed to publish on your behalf, plus basic profile information (user.info.basic). We do not collect data about other TikTok users.

2. How we use it

The tokens are used only to upload and publish videos to your own TikTok account at the times you configure (video.publish). We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored on the device or server where the app runs, in a private file that is not published. Videos are downloaded temporarily to publish and deleted afterwards.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep tokens only while the app is authorized. You can revoke access in your TikTok settings at any time, and ask us to delete any stored data by contacting us.

5. Third parties

The app communicates with TikTok through its official APIs. TikTok's own privacy policy applies to data handled by TikTok.

6. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: samuel@tchstone.net
